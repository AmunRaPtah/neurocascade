# P15.2–P15.4 — human receptor source boundary

The human receptor registry now distinguishes `in-vivo PET` records from
`in-vitro` records. D2, MOR, and GABA-A benzodiazepine sites are reserved for
human PET occupancy calibration in P16.1. Additional targets are represented
as human-scoped but unavailable until their source values and context
corrections are verified.

No rodent affinity or isolated rodent rate constant is promoted to a human
parameter. In-vitro affinity cannot silently become an in-vivo occupancy
parameter; any correction must be explicit, cited, and separately validated.
Consequently P15.2–P15.4 remain evidence-boundary milestones rather than
claims that the receptor layer is clinically calibrated.
