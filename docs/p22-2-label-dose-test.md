Run as pre-registered in `docs/p22-preregistration.md` (P22.1). Every number
below is regenerated by `neurocascade.human.label_dose_result` (C4) and pinned
by `tests/test_label_dose_result.py`.

## Ordering, as git records it

1. P22.1 (the pre-registration) was committed alone.
2. P22.2f (the scoring function) and P22.2a (commit 43b4155: olanzapine wired
   through the chain, predicted-window table pinned in
   `tests/test_label_dose_prediction.py`) were merged with no label range in
   the repository as a number.
3. This task transcribed the two label ranges and scored them.

Haloperidol is **not blind** (pre-declared: its window was published in P19.5,
and P20.6's ledger row had already noted its label). Olanzapine is
procedurally blind. Point 3 of the pre-registration's contamination inventory
still applies: olanzapine's dose range is common clinical knowledge.

## Transcriptions

**Haloperidol**, ANDA216918, effective_time 20260831, set_id
a0751f2e-05ab-4ba9-9bae-7b791bf1a621. Verbatim:

> Oral Administration Initial Dosage Range Adults Moderate Symptomatology -
> 0.5 mg to 2 mg b.i.d. or t.i.d. Severe Symptomatology - 3 mg to 5 mg b.i.d.
> or t.i.d. [...] Chronic or Resistant Patients - 3 mg to 5 mg b.i.d. or
> t.i.d. [...] Daily dosages up to 100 mg may be necessary in some cases to
> achieve an optimal response.

The adult doses are printed only as an "Initial Dosage Range". The rule
excludes initial doses "that the label instructs to increase from". This label
gives no such instruction ("Dosage adjustments, either upward or downward"; at
maintenance, "gradually reduced to the lowest effective maintenance level"), so
the printed ranges are the recommendation. L_low = 0.5 mg b.i.d. = 1.0 mg/day.
Two readings of L_high are defensible: 5 mg t.i.d. = 15 mg/day, the highest
dose printed as a range, and 100 mg/day from the "up to 100 mg" sentence. The
narrower, [1, 15], is scored. The label's "beyond 6 mg per day" sentence is in
the Children section and is not used. The indication is "psychotic disorders",
not schizophrenia (P20.6).

**Olanzapine**, ZYPREXA, NDA020592 (the record also carries NDA021086 Zydis
and NDA021253 IM), effective_time 20260211, set_id
b418946a-1ab4-4d89-a012-d94fc361a3c4, section 2.1 Schizophrenia, Adults.
Verbatim:

> generally beginning with 5 to 10 mg initially, with a target dose of 10
> mg/day within several days. [...] An increase to a dose greater than the
> target dose of 10 mg/day (i.e., to a dose of 15 mg/day or greater) is
> recommended only after clinical assessment. Olanzapine is not indicated for
> use in doses above 20 mg/day. [...] The effectiveness of oral olanzapine, 10
> mg/day to 20 mg/day, in maintaining treatment response [...]

The rule excludes the 5 mg start, because the label says to move to the
10 mg/day target. Acute is 10 (target) to 20 (cap), maintenance is 10 to 20,
and the union is [10, 20]. The acute efficacy range printed from the trials, 10
to 15 mg/day, would not change the union, so there is only one reading.

## Deviations from pre-registration

- **D1. Haloperidol's snapshot does not match its P20.6 pin.** The
  pre-registration says labels are read "from the pinned snapshot (SHA-256
  verified against `fda_sources`)". The haloperidol bytes on disk are 43090
  bytes, sha256 16046778…, and the pin is 43053 bytes, efecfcc2…. The
  set_id, SPL version (3) and effective_time are the same. A live openFDA
  fetch on 2026-09-26 returned exactly the on-disk bytes, so the drift is on
  openFDA's side. The pinned bytes cannot be recovered, so the text that was
  read is pinned on the transcription instead (`sha256_read`), with
  `matches_p20_6_pin=False`. `fda_sources` is left unchanged: it is P20.6's
  record of what P20.6 read. Olanzapine matches its pin. Ziprasidone and
  clozapine also no longer match, which matters when they are transcribed.
- **D2. Refused drugs' labels are not transcribed.** The pre-registration does
  not say whether a drug refused under C2 has its label transcribed. It is not.
  Transcribing a label as a number before a prediction exists for that drug
  would make its future prediction non-blind by the pre-registration's own
  ordering rule. This does not affect any metric: a refused drug scores the
  same either way.

## Where the six non-scored drugs stop

The attribution is the first refusing layer in chain order (active-metabolite,
pk, occupancy). Every refusing layer, with its message, is on
`predict_window(drug)`.

- **pk (4 drugs: paliperidone, quetiapine, ziprasidone, clozapine).** None has
  an oral PK record the chain accepts. The FDA population-PK rows that P21.3
  transcribed for these drugs are refused by `fda_pk_bridge.refusals`, or no
  such row exists. Each of the four is also refused at the occupancy layer,
  because no D2 EC50 is registered. Quetiapine's occupancy refusal is
  structural (P21.2: no plasma EC50 exists in the literature).
- **active-metabolite (risperidone, aripiprazole).** `require_parent_only_path`
  refuses both (P21.4; V6 and V7). Both are also refused at pk and occupancy.

None of these is a miss. The prediction was never made, and C2 forbids reading
either as a pass.

## Reading, and how much the passes are worth

- Both scored drugs pass. A test the model passes 2 of 2 needs its power
  stated. Overlap is weak: haloperidol's scored label range spans 15-fold. The
  twofold midpoint criterion is the part with teeth.
- This paragraph is a sensitivity statement, not a metric. The PK is linear
  and the occupancy is a hyperbola with fixed Emax, so each window dose scales
  exactly with EC50. Olanzapine therefore passes for any EC50 between 0.73x
  and 2.9x the de Greef 2011 value. Haloperidol passes between 0.48x and
  1.93x of its V1 value. The 2–2.5x tracer and region floor recorded in P19.7
  and P21.2 is as wide as these bands, so a pass cannot separate EC50
  candidates within that floor.
- Olanzapine's predicted midpoint, 9.73 mg/day (from the doses rounded to three significant
  figures), is 0.69x the label midpoint,
  14.1 mg/day. The window's lower end, 5.77, is below the 10 mg/day target.
  The model puts 65% mean occupancy at a dose the label treats as a starting
  dose. This is within the criterion. It is not attributed to a layer, because
  the pre-registration defines no miss inside twofold.
- Olanzapine's inputs are one sourced EC50 (de Greef 2011, fitted-free Emax
  0.875) and the LYBALVI population PK (NDA 213378). Neither was fitted in
  this repository, and neither has seen a label dose.
