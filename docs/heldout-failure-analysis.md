# P8.3: held-out failure analysis

This is the standing limitations record for the P8.2 held-out validation. It
is deliberately written as a failure analysis, not as a list of excuses: each
finding names the layer that owns it, the evidence that supports the
attribution, and the data or mechanism needed to remove it.

The numerical snapshot below is from the committed
`results/heldout/heldout_validation.txt`, generated by
`scripts/run_heldout_validation.py`. The report is authoritative for the
numbers; this document records what they mean and what must happen next.

## Result in one paragraph

There are seven compounds in the held-out registry and none has a complete
dose-to-behaviour chain. All seven have a missing receptor-drivable
plasma-to-brain-unbound partition, and none has a usable dose-to-concentration
PK model for the observed route/species pairs. Five published occupancy
observations therefore cannot be scored. Only alprazolam has three
quantitative dose levels with a dispersion, so its reachable-range result is
not a fitted success: all three observations are reachable only by choosing an
unidentified gain. The only structural behavioural refutation is alprazolam's
non-monotone anxiety series, which exceeds 1 standard deviation but not 1.96.

The validation is consequently a result about model readiness and literature
coverage, not a claim that the seven compounds' behavioural magnitudes have
been predicted.

## Failure matrix

| Layer | Evidence in P8.2 | Attribution | Concrete fix and required data |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dose-to-concentration PK | All seven compounds fail the PK link for their observed routes, or have no observed route on which to build a model. | Upstream PK coverage failure. A dose cannot be compared with a concentration-state model without route-specific `F`, `ka`, `Vd`, and `CL` (and disposition terms where needed). | Add one species/route parameter set at a time from measured concentration-time data, including administered dose, body weight, route, sampling times, plasma/blood matrix, `F`, `ka`, `Vd`, `CL`, and uncertainty. Validate Cmax, Tmax, AUC, and disposition phases before using the set for prediction. |
| Plasma-to-brain partition | Every held-out compound is missing `Kp,uu` or an equivalent receptor-drivable partition in the rat status query. | Upstream brain-exposure failure. Total brain:plasma `Kp` is not an unbound receptor concentration and must not be substituted. | Obtain paired plasma and brain extracellular/unbound measurements in the same species, route, dose and time window, or measure `Kp`, `fu,plasma`, and `fu,brain` sufficiently to derive `Kp,uu`. Record assay and uncertainty. |
| Occupancy tier | Five occupancy observations exist, but zero can be scored because PK and brain partition are both missing. | Not an occupancy-model failure. The receptor equation is not testable until the two upstream concentration links close. | For each occupancy study, source matched PK and `Kp,uu`; otherwise run a paired occupancy-plus-concentration experiment. Do not convert total tissue occupancy into a receptor-driving concentration by assumption. |
| Binding provenance | Caffeine and cocaine occupancy data are mouse while the carried affinity is rat; the ketamine NMDA entry is explicitly probable rather than confirmed. | Binding/species alignment limitation. These are identifiable confounds in a future occupancy comparison, not evidence that the receptor equation is wrong. | Use same-species, same-preparation affinity and functional data for the validation compound, or carry a species/preparation uncertainty model that is independently measured. Confirm ketamine's receptor/site and radioligand instead of retaining the probable assignment. |
| Transporter/projection layer | DAT, NET and SERT are explicitly reported as having no whole-brain behavioural field. Cocaine is therefore scored through an available M1 path, while amphetamine's available alpha2 path does not represent transporter-mediated monoamine release. | Mechanism reachability failure. The transporter binding fingerprint cannot yet reach the receptor fields and endpoints through the model's projection system. Cocaine's M1 direction mismatch is especially likely to be a wrong available path rather than evidence that M1 mediates cocaine locomotion. | Close the transporter-to-transmitter-to-receptor loop: measured release/uptake kinetics, source-nucleus activity, projection densities, region-wise transmitter concentrations, endogenous tone, and target-receptor occupancy. Score cocaine and amphetamine through that path only. |
| Endogenous transmitter tone | Caffeine at A2A and ketamine at NMDA are predicted as no-change because the repository has no measured basal adenosine or glutamate tone for those targets, while the published observations show effects. | P6.2 coverage failure. A blocker at a tone-free site is structurally inert in this model; the result is a declared boundary condition, not a calibrated drug failure. | Measure basal extracellular adenosine and glutamate by region and state, connect them to release and clearance, and model competition/channel context. Re-run the no-change predictions after the tone field exists. |
| Behavioural evidence | Direction is scored for 15 observations with 4 matches; only three alprazolam observations have both a ratio and dispersion. The remaining observations are plotted-only, text-only, unverified, or lack a vehicle/error bar. | Evidence-format and assay comparability failure. Most observations cannot support a quantitative residual, and route/species/assay differences can reverse an endpoint's interpretation. | Curate matched vehicle and drug groups with dose, route, species, timing, assay definition, mean, dispersion and sample size. Require at least three comparable dose levels before testing shape or fitting magnitude. |
| Behaviour readout shape | Alprazolam anxiety rises after falling: the turnover is resolved at 1 SD but not at 1.96 SD. | Structural readout failure at suggestive, not decisive, strength. The current monotone dose→activation→endpoint composition cannot produce an inverted-U at any gain. | First replicate the turnover with a sufficiently powered, route-matched study. If confirmed, add a mechanism supported by data—receptor reserve/desensitization, active metabolite, state dependence, or an assay-specific decoder—and compare it out of sample. Do not add a generic polynomial merely to pass this series. |
| Circuit gains and endpoint magnitudes | Alprazolam's three quantitative observations are all inside the reachable range, but the implied gain is unidentified and the residual is zero by construction. Most whole-brain gains remain illustrative. | Identifiability failure, not successful prediction. A reachable observation only proves that some gain can reproduce it. | Fit gains only after PK, partition and matched behavioural data are available; report posterior uncertainty and rank/identifiability. Keep a zero residual labelled `reachable-gain-unidentified`, never `validated`. |
| Whole-brain numerical reachability | Several D2, GABA-A and alpha2 endpoint sweeps are marked non-converged at the outer gain range. | Numerical/reporting limitation in the saturation sweep, separate from the pharmacology failures. | Stabilize the range calculation and benchmark solver tolerances before using an endpoint's asymptotic range as a hard refutation. This belongs in Phase 9, with a reference-solver comparison. |

## What is and is not a model failure

The 4/15 direction agreement is not a single scalar score of the project. It
mixes three different situations:

1. Alprazolam has a reachable, sign-correct anxiety point and a structural
   turnover that the current monotone model cannot reproduce.
2. Caffeine and ketamine are routed to targets with no measured endogenous
   tone, so their `no-change` predictions are consequences of an explicitly
   incomplete P6.2 field.
3. Cocaine and the transporter component of amphetamine lack a whole-brain
   transporter path. A direction attributed through a remaining receptor path
   is not evidence that that receptor is the drug's behavioural mechanism.

Only the first item is presently a direct structural challenge to the
behavioural readout. The others are reachability and data-join failures that
must be fixed before a quantitative drug score is interpretable.

## Repair order

The shortest honest route to a real held-out prediction is:

1. Pick one held-out compound and one rodent route with both a concentration-
   time study and a receptor-drivable `Kp,uu` study. Add and validate those
   two links without borrowing another drug's parameters.
2. Re-run occupancy first. Occupancy is the cleanest test because it has no
   circuit gain or endpoint weight in the chain.
3. Add matched behavioural data for that same species, route and exposure
   window, then fit only the gains that the data identifies.
4. Extend the transporter/projection and endogenous-tone fields before
   interpreting cocaine, amphetamine, caffeine or ketamine behaviour.
5. Address the alprazolam turnover only if it survives a replicated,
   uncertainty-resolved study; then compare a mechanistic non-monotone model
   against the current monotone baseline out of sample.
6. Resolve the non-converged asymptotic sweeps in Phase 9 before treating a
   reachable-range boundary as a definitive structural refutation.

Until step 2 is complete for a compound, the project should describe that
compound as *chain-incomplete*, not as quantitatively validated or falsified.

## Standing limitations to preserve

- Never substitute fentanyl, diazepam or haloperidol PK for a held-out drug.
- Never treat total brain:plasma `Kp` as `Kp,uu`.
- Never call a reachable observation a prediction when the gain was selected
  to reproduce it.
- Keep missing, unsearched and species-mismatched evidence distinct.
- Keep a transporter ligand out of the behavioural path until the
  transporter/projection field is implemented.
- Preserve negative results in `results/heldout/` when better data removes a
  blocker; the old result explains what changed.

The source report, CSV tables and figures are in
`results/heldout/`; the executable guards for the result are in
`tests/test_heldout*.py`.
