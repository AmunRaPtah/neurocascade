The two networks
----------------
  Track C1 (hand baselines): drug-free fixed point has VTA_DA at
  4.90e-06, i.e. saturated OFF, so the VTA_DA -> NAc projection carries
  nothing at rest and a PRESYNAPTIC modulator of it cannot act.

  stabilized network: 7 nodes
    operating gain rho = 0.9 (ILLUSTRATIVE)
    weight_scale = 0.342003   (= rho / kappa, kappa = 2.63156)
    contraction number after scaling: 0.9 < 1, unique globally attracting fixed point
    baseline_input: balanced to r* = [0.5]


Each cell is the signed extremum of (dosed - drug free) over the
40-minute run. 'change' is (mu+kappa+delta) against (mu only), as a
percentage of the mu-only effect. n/a where the ligand does not
exist in the mu-only configuration.

NALOXONE REVERSAL POTENCY, mu against kappa
-------------------------------------------
The consequence of a 1.79-fold selectivity, made into a number.
Naloxone concentration (nM) needed to halve an established
effect, by search over a log ladder, on the stabilized network.
The mu effect is read on analgesia_index (PAG, which only the mu
system touches) and the kappa effect on accumbal_drive.

agonist                      readout              effect alone  naloxone_50
fentanyl 10 nM (mu)          analgesia_index           0.34684        177.8
U50488 100 nM (kappa)        accumbal_drive           -0.20641       1000.0
dynorphin A 1000 nM (kappa)  accumbal_drive           -0.11245       1000.0

  These are circuit-level numbers and they mix three things:
  naloxone's own selectivity, the two agonists' different
  affinities for their own receptors, and the different shapes of
  the two readouts. The pharmacological version below removes the
  last two.

The same question at the receptor, with the circuit removed
-----------------------------------------------------------
Naloxone concentration halving the AGONIST's peak occupancy, with
each agonist dosed at 7.5x its own Kd so the two competitions
start from the same place. This isolates naloxone.

agonist / receptor        Kd (nM)  dose (nM)     peak occ  naloxone_50
fentanyl at mu              1.346     10.095       0.8663        10.00
U50488 at kappa             0.200      1.500       0.8265        13.34

  ratio kappa / mu = 1.33, against a naloxone Ki ratio of 1.79.

  That is the brief's sentence as a number. A model that treated
  naloxone as a mu-selective antagonist would put these two
  concentrations orders of magnitude apart; measured, they are
  within a small factor, because 1.4 nM and 2.5 nM are within a
  small factor. What this model still cannot show is the
  clinically consequential half of it -- that a reversal dose also
  strips ENDOGENOUS dynorphin off kappa -- because P6.2 has
  supplied no tone, so the kappa agonist is injected by hand here.
