  NET is the only one of the three built in the first-order form, and
  that is a gap rather than a preference: what is published for it is a
  clearance half-life, which fixes Vmax/Km and neither of them. Splitting
  a measured ratio into two numbers would be inventing one.

  The dopamine pool relaxes with a time constant of Km/Vmax = 51.61 ms,
  more than three orders of magnitude below the circuit's. That is why
  every run in this module defaults to LSODA, and a test asserts that
  RK45 agrees.

2. The five drugs, and what one micromolar of each does
========================================================================

  Extracellular transmitter at steady state as a multiple of its own
  drug-free level. For a pure blocker this is EXACTLY 1 + C/Ki: the mass
  balance cancels Km, Vmax and the basal level, so this column depends on
  one measured constant per drug and on nothing illustrative.

  drug              site      Ki (nM)      1 uM ->   source
  ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  cocaine           DAT         490.0        3.04x   [HanGu2006] mouse
  cocaine           SERT        730.0        2.37x   [HanGu2006] mouse
  cocaine           NET         460.0        3.17x   [HanGu2006] mouse
  methylphenidate   DAT         260.0        4.85x   [HanGu2006] mouse
  methylphenidate   SERT     114370.0        1.01x   [HanGu2006] mouse
  methylphenidate   NET         170.0        6.88x   [HanGu2006] mouse
  amphetamine       DAT         560.0  see block 3   [HanGu2006] mouse
  amphetamine       SERT      23820.0  see block 3   [HanGu2006] mouse
  amphetamine       NET         120.0  see block 3   [HanGu2006] mouse
  fluoxetine        DAT          none            -   GAP, see below
  fluoxetine        SERT          3.0      334.33x   [Tatsumi1997] human
  fluoxetine        NET          none            -   GAP, see below
  atomoxetine       DAT        1451.0        1.69x   [Bymaster2002] human
  atomoxetine       SERT         77.0       13.99x   [Bymaster2002] human
  atomoxetine       NET           5.0      201.00x   [Bymaster2002] human

  The gaps, recorded rather than filled:
    fluoxetine at DAT
      No equilibrium constant obtained, same reason. Bounded omission:
      [JohnJones2007] measured an apparent Ki of 270 uM against dopamine
      uptake in mouse tissue, so the action left out is one there is
      independent evidence is negligible.
    fluoxetine at NET
      No equilibrium constant obtained. [Tatsumi1997] measured it and
      the table was not reachable; IUPHAR curates only that paper's SERT
      cell. Unbounded omission: fluoxetine's noradrenergic action is
      absent from this model and nothing here bounds its size.

  Two numbers in that table are OVER-PREDICTIONS and are reported as
  such: fluoxetine at 334x and atomoxetine at 201x. They are what an
  open-loop mass balance gives when nothing lets a rising transmitter
  cut its own release. Real 5-HT1A and alpha2 autoreceptors do exactly
  that. This is the sharpest statement in this codebase so far of what
  the projection-system layer (P6.1, P6.2) is for.

========================================================================
3. Substrate versus blocker
========================================================================

  Cocaine, methylphenidate, fluoxetine and atomoxetine bind the
  transporter and are not carried across it. Amphetamine is carried
  across, and drives transmitter back out by reverse transport, which no
  blocker does ([Rothman2012]'s substrate-reversal criterion, [Zhu2008]).
  In this model that is two constants rather than an adjective.

  site    release EC50 (nM)      block Ki (nM)      ratio       Emax
  ------------------------------------------------------------------
  DAT                   6.4                560      87.5x       1.03
  SERT               1960.0              23820      12.2x       1.04
  NET                   7.4                120      16.2x       1.01

  Amphetamine's releasing action at DAT is 88-fold more potent than its
  blocking action, measured in two different laboratories' assays. A
  model representing it as a blocker alone would put its dopaminergic
  potency nearly two orders of magnitude too low.

  Simulated, 100 nM amphetamine, with the release mechanism on and off:

                                   DA       5-HT         NA
  substrate + blocker           2.39x      1.06x      3.54x
  blocker only (wrong)          1.18x      1.00x      1.82x

  RELEASE_CAPACITY, the absolute efflux a saturating substrate drives, is
  this module's one illustrative mechanism constant; the ORDERING above
  holds for every positive value of it, and a test says so.

4. Provenance checks
========================================================================

  a. Two MOUSE preparations, cloned transporters against brain slices
     ([HanGu2006] against [JohnJones2007]), fold disagreement:

       amphetamine       DAT           1.33x
       amphetamine       SERT          1.04x
       cocaine           DAT           1.40x
       cocaine           SERT         13.70x
       fluoxetine        SERT      19666.67x
       methylphenidate   DAT           1.24x
       methylphenidate   SERT          6.02x

     The three DAT cells agree within 1.4-fold: a cloned mouse
     transporter in a gut-derived cell line and a mouse striatal terminal
     in a slice give the same number. The SERT cells do not, and
     [JohnJones2007] states the caveat itself. Fluoxetine is a different
     world entirely, and the module docstring argues which value to use.

  b. Two SPECIES from one paper's own two columns ([HanGu2006]):

       amphetamine       DAT           1.14x
       amphetamine       NET           1.71x
       amphetamine       SERT          1.61x
       cocaine           DAT           2.13x
       cocaine           NET           1.04x
       cocaine           SERT          1.01x
       methylphenidate   DAT           4.33x
       methylphenidate   NET           1.70x
       methylphenidate   SERT          1.16x

     Eight of nine under 2.4-fold. This is what using human constants
     for fluoxetine and atomoxetine costs, measured rather than hoped.

  c. Two LABORATORIES on atomoxetine ([Bymaster2002], [Carlier1998]):

       DAT           1.34x   (1451.0 nM against 1080.00 nM)
       NET           2.46x   (   5.0 nM against   2.03 nM)
       SERT          8.65x   (  77.0 nM against   8.90 nM)

     The SERT cell is the one that matters: NET-over-SERT selectivity is
     15.4-fold on the numbers used here and 4.4-fold on the other paper's.
     Between-laboratory spread is LARGER here than the species spread in
     (b), which is the useful ordering.

5. The Phase 6 interface, run
========================================================================

  The calibrated D2 site, unchanged, with its `dopamine` ligand driven by
  DAT's extracellular pool instead of by a dose injected by hand. The
  interface is one dict entry: transmitter_sources={'dopamine': 'DAT'}.

  Endogenous tone, from two measurements and no fitting: [Bosse2011]'s
  basal 5.0 nM against [Agren2021]'s D2 Kd of 2.03 nM puts the receptor
  at 71.1% occupancy before any drug.

  condition                DA (nM)    D2 occ  halo occ   locomotor
  ----------------------------------------------------------------
  no drug                     5.00     0.711     0.000   0.5000 (+0.0000)
  cocaine 1 uM               10.10     0.833     0.000   0.6650 (+0.1650)
  methylphenidate 1 uM       14.62     0.878     0.000   0.6811 (+0.1811)
  amphetamine 100 nM         10.73     0.841     0.000   0.6652 (+0.1652)
  atomoxetine 1 uM            6.72     0.768     0.000   0.6089 (+0.1089)
  fluoxetine 1 uM             5.00     0.711     0.000   0.5000 (+0.0000)
  haloperidol 100 nM          5.00     0.008     0.989   0.3000 (-0.2000)

  Three readings, and the third is new to this codebase:
    * the psychostimulants raise the readout, by raising extracellular
      dopamine at a receptor that was given no dose of dopamine;
    * fluoxetine does nothing here, correctly: it has no measured
      constant at DAT and this circuit has no serotonin receptor;
    * haloperidol given ALONE moves the circuit. Every module from P5.1
      to P5.7 had to write 'an antagonist alone is inert here, because
      there is no endogenous transmitter to displace'. There is now, for
      dopamine, and it is a measured 5.0 nM.

  What is still missing is the loop back: nothing lets the circuit's own
  firing rate set the release rate. That is P6.1, and
  MonoamineTransporter.release_rate is a plain settable attribute exactly
  so that P6.1 can replace it without touching this mechanism.
