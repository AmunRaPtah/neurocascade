Independent cross-checks, carried rather than reconciled:
  dopamine       2.38 nM [PanLai1995], 0.48x the value used
      conscious rat medial PFC by zero net flux, 2.38 +/- 0.70 nM: a
      DIFFERENT REGION rather than a disagreement, and the only measured
      dopamine concentration in this codebase from outside the striatum.
      [Ihalainen1999] puts mouse striatal dopamine 13-30 fold above
      cortical and hippocampal dopamine in dialysate, which is the
      ordering P6.3's density map has to reproduce and which this
      2.1-fold cross-region ratio does NOT reproduce.
  serotonin      (none found)
  noradrenaline  14 nM [Abercrombie1988], 8.81x the value used
      rat hippocampus, dialysate corrected for a 21% in vitro recovery,
      ~14 nM: 8.8-fold above the value used. In vitro recovery exceeds
      in vivo recovery, so a value corrected by it is biased UP, and
      zero net flux is the method this codebase prefers; the smaller
      value is used and the disagreement is reported rather than split.
  acetylcholine  4 nM [DeBundel2008], 0.22x the value used
      rat hippocampus: 0.40 +/- 0.12 nM in dialysate at an in vivo
      recovery the authors put at ~10%, i.e. ~4 nM extracellular,
      4.6-fold below the value used. The dialysate number itself is a
      strict LOWER BOUND on the extracellular concentration, because
      dialysate = extraction fraction * extracellular and the extraction
      fraction is at most 1.
  histamine      (none found)

Caveats, attached to each constant:
  dopamine:
    * wild-type controls of a BDNF-deficient study, 5.0 +/- 0.2 nM
  serotonin:
    * wild-type SERT(+/+), 2.8 +/- 1 nM; the same paper's frontal
      cortex value is 1.4 +/- 0.4 nM
  noradrenaline:
    * RAT, where dopamine's and serotonin's basal levels here are
      mouse
    * the conscious group is used; the same paper's anaesthetized
      groups read 4.38 +/- 1.39 and 3.67 +/- 0.90 nM and are
      deliberately not used, because anaesthesia halved the extraction
      fraction in the paper's own finding
    * no mouse value was found; that gap is still open
  acetylcholine:
    * RAT, not mouse
    * STRIATUM, where extracellular acetylcholine comes from tonically
      active interneurons rather than from the three projection nuclei
      this codebase's acetylcholine field is built on. A source
      mismatch as well as a region mismatch; see the module docstring
    * large dispersion: +/- 11.8 nM on 18.4 nM with n = 10
  histamine:
    * RAT, not mouse
    * the hypothalamus is the tuberomammillary nucleus's own
      neighbourhood rather than a forebrain target
    * recovery-corrected dialysate rather than zero net flux
    * [Cumming1991] found striatal extracellular histamine
      tetrodotoxin-insensitive and only 20% calcium-dependent, so part
      of what a probe collects may not be neuronal

Citations
------------------------------------------------------------------------
[Abercrombie1988] Abercrombie ED, Keller RW Jr, Zigmond MJ. Characterization of hippocampal norepinephrine release as measured by microdialysis perfusion: pharmacological and behavioral studies. Neuroscience 1988;27:897-904, PMID:3252176. Freely-moving rat hippocampus: 'Correcting for recovery (21%), the concentration of norepinephrine in the extracellular fluid of hippocampus under resting conditions was estimated to be approximately 14 nM'. The cross-check on [PanLai1995], 8.8-fold above it. Its closing sentence is also the clearest statement of this phase's own premise: 'this suggests that nerve terminal density is an important factor in determining the concentration of catecholamines in the extracellular fluid'.

[Bosse2011] Bosse KE et al. J Neurochem 2011;120:385-395, PMC3385875. Mouse basal extracellular dopamine by zero net flux, 5.0 +/- 0.2 nM.

[DeBundel2008] De Bundel D, Sarre S, Van Eeckhaut A, Smolders I, Michotte Y. Critical evaluation of acetylcholine determination in rat brain microdialysates using ion-pair liquid chromatography with amperometric detection. Sensors 2008;8:5171-5185, PMC3705495. 'The concentration of acetylcholine in 30 uL microdialysates obtained under basal conditions from the hippocampus of freely moving rats was 0.40 +/- 0.12 nM (mean +/- SD, n = 30)', with the authors' own estimate that 'the in vivo recovery for acetylcholine in microdialysis samples was approximately 10%'. The independent check on [Vinson1997], 4.6-fold below it.

[Ihalainen1999] Ihalainen JA, Riekkinen P Jr, Feenstra MG. Comparison of dopamine and noradrenaline release in mouse prefrontal cortex, striatum and hippocampus using microdialysis. Neurosci Lett 1999;277:71-74. MOUSE, dialysate rather than recovery-corrected: 'Basal release of NA was similar in these areas, but DA in striatum was 13-30 times higher than in other areas'. Consumed by no equation; it is the regional ORDERING that P6.3's density map has to reproduce.

[Itoh1991] Itoh Y, Oishi R, Nishibori M, Saeki K. Characterization of histamine release from the rat hypothalamus as measured by in vivo microdialysis. J Neurochem 1991;56:769-774. Anaesthetized rat, medial hypothalamus: 'The basal HA output was almost constant ... the mean value being 7.1 pg/30 min'; 'extracellular HA concentration was assumed to be 7.8 nM, by a calculation from in vitro recovery through the dialysis membrane'. Potassium-evoked release was calcium-dependent (+170%).

[Mathews2004] Mathews TA et al. J Neurosci Methods 2004;140:169-181. Mouse basal extracellular serotonin by zero net flux, 2.8 +/- 1 nM in striatum.

[PanLai1995] Pan WH, Lai YJ. Anesthetics decreased the microdialysis extraction fraction of norepinephrine but not dopamine in the medial prefrontal cortex. Synapse 1995;21:85-92, PMID:8525466. Conscious rat medial prefrontal cortex by ZERO NET FLUX: noradrenaline 1.59 +/- 0.37 nM at 51 +/- 8% in vivo recovery, dopamine 2.38 +/- 0.70 nM at 41 +/- 6%. Added by P6.2; the source of `monoamine_transporters.BASAL_NORADRENALINE`, and the only measured DOPAMINE concentration in this codebase from a region other than the striatum.

[Vinson1997] Vinson PN, Justice JB Jr. Effect of neostigmine on concentration and extraction fraction of acetylcholine using quantitative microdialysis. J Neurosci Methods 1997;73:61-67, PMID:9130679. Rat STRIATUM, no-net-flux quantitative microdialysis: 'The control ACh concentration was determined to be 18.4 +/- 11.8 nM (n = 10; mean +/- S.E.M.)' with no cholinesterase inhibitor in the perfusate, rising linearly to 581 +/- 109 nM under 300 nM neostigmine. Added by P6.2; the source of `ACETYLCHOLINE_BASAL`.
