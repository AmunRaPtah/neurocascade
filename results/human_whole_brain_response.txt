------------------------------------------------------------------------
What this result is, and what it is not
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  The connectome and the expression map are human data, so the PATTERN - thalamus and accumbens among the largest responders, every OPRM1-bearing node suppressed - is a data-driven human result.
  The MAGNITUDE is not calibrated: the mechanism gain is the mouse scenarios' illustrative MU_OPIOID_MECHANISM_GAIN (no human gain has ever been fitted), the node parameters are the illustrative mouse defaults, and rho = 0.9 is the illustrative operating gain. A reader must not read the numbers above as a prediction of how far a human brain moves.
