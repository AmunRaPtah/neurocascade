========================================================================
Receptor maps against independently known distributions (P4.3)
========================================================================

Generated by scripts/report_receptor_map_validation.py.
Expression policy for every number below: plane='coronal-else-sagittal', combine='mean'.

  These claims are bug detectors, not findings. A concordant claim
  that fails means the ingestion or the normalization is wrong. Claims
  are ordinal only - min(high) > max(low) - because P4.2 measured this
  conversion to be a good ordering (Spearman ~0.71 against mouse D1
  autoradiography) and a bad scale (proportional R2 0.31, log-log
  slope 0.46). A claim is ASSERTED by the test suite only when its
  source separates the two groups by at least 2 of the source's own
  tiers; single-tier claims are REPORTED here and their outcome
  printed either way.

5. Are the Drd1 and Drd2 rows the anatomy they claim to be?
------------------------------------------------------------------------

  The one cross-gene check, and the only legitimate shape for one: a
  ratio of within-gene ratios, so the per-experiment probe and
  segmentation differences that make expression energy incomparable
  between genes cancel instead of being ignored. Well above 1 means
  Drd2 is midbrain-weighted relative to Drd1, which is the
  D2-autoreceptor anatomy [bello2011]; near 1 would mean the two gene
  rows had been shuffled into each other.

7. What this validation does not claim
------------------------------------------------------------------------

  It does not claim the maps are right. Ordinal agreement with six
  cited distributions rules out the failure modes that matter for an
  ingestion (a transposed matrix, a shifted gene column, a
  structure-id mis-join, a normalization along the wrong axis) and
  rules out nothing about the mRNA-to-protein step, which P4.2
  measured separately and found to be worth a factor of 172 for the
  dopamine transporter. Two of the claims here are expected to fail
  for exactly that reason and are asserted to fail.
