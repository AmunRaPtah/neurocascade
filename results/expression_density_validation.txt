Expression energy as a receptor density: what it is worth (P4.2)
========================================================================

THE ASSUMPTION UNDER TEST

  Within one gene, receptor density in a region is assumed
  proportional to Allen ISH expression energy in that region: density
  = k_gene * energy, k_gene unknown and per gene. mRNA is not protein,
  protein is not binding sites, and for the genes in
  mismatch.MISMATCHES the protein is not even in the same region as
  the transcript. This is an assumption, not a measurement;
  validation.py measures its error where an independent mouse density
  map exists.

Generated by scripts/report_density_validation.py.
Expression policy for every number below: plane='coronal-else-sagittal', combine='mean'.

  published regions NOT in the comparison, and why:
    Nucleus accumbens core
      no CCF counterpart: CCF's ACB is undivided, so core and shell
      would both join to it; merging them here would be an averaging
      rule invented by this module. `ACCUMBENS_SUBDIVISIONS` records
      the two values instead.
    Nucleus accumbens shell
      no CCF counterpart: the other half of the same problem: CCF's
      ACB is one structure and this paper measured two, so joining
      either one to ACB would be picking a subdivision to stand for
      the whole
    Nigrostriatal bundle
      no CCF counterpart: a fibre tract. Its D1 binding is receptor
      protein in axonal transit, which is the mechanism `mismatch.py`
      is about, and it is precisely why a transcript map cannot have a
      counterpart for it.
    Amygdala
      no CCF counterpart: not one CCF structure: the amygdalar nuclei
      are split between CTXsp (BLA, LA, BMA, PA) and sAMY (CEA, MEA,
      IA), and choosing a subset to stand for 'amygdala' would be
      inventing the join
    Substantia nigra
      no CCF counterpart: CCF has SNc and SNr as siblings with no
      combined parent, and the two differ by an order of magnitude in
      Drd1 and Slc6a3 transcript. `SUBSTANTIA_NIGRA_ALTERNATIVES`
      carries the value against both, and `mismatch.py` uses it as the
      terminal-field case rather than burying it in a correlation.

  fitted absolute scale: k_Drd1 = 17 fmol/mg tissue equivalent per expression-energy unit
    fitted on 10 regions, R2 +0.310. The R2 travels with the constant
    because a k fitted this loosely is a real number and a bad one.

  the same comparison with 'substantia nigra' joined to SNr:
    n = 11, Spearman rho +0.591 (p = 0.05558), proportional R2 +0.147
    SNr is the single largest disagreement in the whole comparison
    and it is not a measurement problem; see section 3.

3. Where the transcript map puts the receptor in the wrong region
------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Measured cases (both sides quantified in mouse):

    Slc6a3: transcript puts 120 times as much in SNr as in CP;
    [3H]mazindol binding puts 0.692 times as much. Assuming density is
    proportional to transcript misstates the SNr:CP ratio by a factor of
    173.
      transcript  SNr      7.121   CP       0.060
      [3H]mazindol SNr       77.1   CP       111.4  yoo2010

    Drd1: transcript puts 63.2 times as much in CP as in SNr;
    [3H]SCH-23390 binding puts 1.98 times as much. Assuming density is
    proportional to transcript misstates the CP:SNr ratio by a factor of
    31.9.
      transcript  CP      14.494   SNr      0.229
      [3H]SCH-23390 CP       409.2   SNr      206.3  yoo2010

  Documented but not quantified (no mouse table with both sides):

    Cnr1 [terminal-field]  CP/HIP/Isocortex -> SNr/GPe/GPi
    Slc6a4 [terminal-field]  DR/RAmb -> Isocortex/STR/HIP/TH
    Slc6a2 [terminal-field]  LC -> Isocortex/HIP/TH
    Oprm1 [partial]  MH -> IPN
    Adra2a [partial]  LC/Isocortex -> Isocortex/HIP
    Drd2 [partial]  CP/ACB/SNc/VTA -> CP/ACB

  Genes deliberately NOT in the registry: the ionotropic receptors
  (Grin*, Gria*, Gabra*, Gabrb*), which are predominantly
  postsynaptic on the dendrites of the cell that transcribes them.
  Their absence is an assertion about them, not an oversight.

5. Receptors with no usable density data
------------------------------------------------------------------------

  mu-opioid (Oprm1)
    why wanted:
      Oprm1 is the receptor of this codebase's fentanyl scenarios, so
      it is the single most valuable comparison available - more
      valuable than D1, which no scenario currently uses.
    searched:
      2026-08-12. Kitchen I, Slowe SJ, Matthes HW, Kieffer B.
      'Quantitative autoradiographic mapping of mu-, delta- and
      kappa-opioid receptors in knockout mice lacking the mu-opioid
      receptor gene.' Brain Res. 1997;778(1):73-88, PMID 9462879, is
      the canonical region-wise mouse [3H]DAMGO table and is behind an
      Elsevier paywall with no PMC deposit. A Europe PMC open-access
      full-text search for DAMGO + autoradiography + mouse + fmol/mg
      returned 14 records; the only one with region-wise mouse MOPr
      densities is Effah F, Nidadavolu P, de Gusmao Taveiros Silva NK,
      Wojtowicz M, Camarini R, Zimmer A, Bailey A. 'Differential
      temporal decline of cerebral oxytocin and mu-opioid receptor
      density during the aging process in mice.' Eur J Neurosci.
      2024;60(11):6686-6703, PMC11612844, which prints its per-region
      values only as bar figures; its one numeric MOPr table is
      whole-brain means by age group.
    conclusion:
      No Oprm1 comparison is made. The abundant RAT mu-opioid
      autoradiography was deliberately not substituted: a rat density
      map compared against a mouse transcript map would measure the
      species difference as if it were the conversion's error. What
      Oprm1 has instead is P4.1's qualitative anatomy check (medial
      habenula, then midline thalamus) and an unquantified
      terminal-field record in mismatch.py.

  D2 outside the striatum (Drd2)
    why wanted:
      Drd2 is the receptor of the haloperidol scenario, and the four
      regions Yoo et al. report are all striatal, so the comparison
      has no dynamic range.
    searched:
      2026-08-12. Yoo et al. 2010 state that [3H]raclopride binding
      outside the striatum was below their film's usable range, which
      is a property of the ligand and the method rather than of that
      study. No open-access mouse table with extrastriatal D2
      densities in comparable units was found alongside it.
    conclusion:
      Drd2 gets an ordering check on two joinable regions (CP above OT
      in both transcript and binding) and no correlation. Two points
      is not a correlation and none is computed.

6. How much of the disagreement is measurement noise
------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Yoo et al. measured three mouse genetic backgrounds on the same
  films. The spread between them is a floor on how precisely any
  single published density number can be read:

    D1   median max/min across backgrounds 1.38, worst Substantia nigra 1.97
    D2   median max/min across backgrounds 1.25, worst Nucleus accumbens shell 1.42
    DAT  median max/min across backgrounds 1.30, worst Ventral tegmental area 1.82

  Against that, the proportional model's per-region errors in
  section 1 run to a factor of 29. The disagreement is not noise.
